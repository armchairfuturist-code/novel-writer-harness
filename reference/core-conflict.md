# Core Philosophical Conflict: Complexity Capacity

## The Problem (2020s)
Human consciousness has a single-threaded bottleneck. Meditation, psychedelics, breathwork, spiritual awakening — these were the only tools for escaping attentional decay and information overload. You could only quiet the noise, not expand the channel.

## The Divide Begins (mid-2020s)
Technologists realized: the same principles we use to build AI — agent separation, spinning off sub-agents and "ghosts" to handle parallel work — could be applied to the human mind itself.

AI development became a **recursive improvement loop not just for AI, but for humans**.

## Two Camps

### Accelerationists / Attentionalists
"We can have the best of all worlds — we can actually augment our minds to handle complexity in real terms, instead of defaulting back to purely spiritual domains of single, focused conscious experience."

The mind is capable of much more if we apply agentic principles to it. Make humans **actually multi-threaded** — able to parse multiple kinds of experience simultaneously.

### Puritans / Ascendants
Oppose this on principle. Making humans multi-threaded, able to split consciousness and hold multiple parallel experiences, is an **abomination**. The single, focused conscious self is sacred and must remain intact.

## Irony/Contradictions
Both sides end up resembling each other in their purity, much like far-left and far-right politics converge in their end goals. The Attentionalists create hierarchy among augmented types. The Puritans use technology to enforce their anti-tech stance.
