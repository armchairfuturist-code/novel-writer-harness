# Dialogue Craft

## Character Voice Differentiation

Each character should have a recognizable speech pattern:
- **Word choice**: vocabulary level, slang, technical terms
- **Sentence structure**: short/long, simple/complex
- **Rhythm**: fast talker vs deliberate speaker
- **Tics**: repeated phrases, filler words, gestures tagged to speech
- **What they don't say**: each character has topics they avoid

## Subtext

The best dialogue operates on two levels:
- **Surface**: what the character says
- **Subtext**: what the character means
- The gap between them is where tension lives

## Dialogue Attribution

- "Said" is invisible - use it 80 percent of the time
- Action beats replace dialogue tags: "She set down the glass. 'I'm leaving.'"
- Avoid: "he exclaimed", "she opined", "he retorted", "she breathed"
- Never use a verb that isn't a speech verb: "he laughed a reply" is wrong
