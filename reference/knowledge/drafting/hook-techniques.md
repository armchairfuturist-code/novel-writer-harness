# Chapter Hook Techniques

## Chapter-End Hooks (13 patterns)

1. **Cliffhanger**: Physical danger left unresolved
2. **Revelation**: New information that recontextualizes everything
3. **Question**: A mystery posed that demands answering
4. **Decision**: Character must choose, choice deferred
5. **Arrival**: Someone or something arrives unexpectedly
6. **Departure**: Character leaves, destination unknown
7. **Threat**: A new danger announced
8. **Promise**: A commitment that will be tested
9. **Reversal**: Situation flips (ally becomes enemy, victory becomes defeat)
10. **Convergence**: Two plot threads collide
11. **Threshold**: Character crosses a point of no return
12. **Discovery**: Finding something that changes the quest
13. **Echo**: Callback to an earlier moment with new meaning

## Chapter-Open Hooks (7 patterns)

1. **In medias res**: Start in the middle of action
2. **Puzzle**: Open with something that doesn't make sense yet
3. **Voice**: Open with a distinctive narrative voice
4. **Image**: Open with a striking visual
5. **Contradiction**: Open with two things that can't both be true
6. **Rule**: Open by stating a rule that will be broken
7. **Forecast**: Open by hinting at what's to come
